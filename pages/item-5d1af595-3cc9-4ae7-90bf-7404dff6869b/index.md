Edge flow is the way a mesh's edge loops run over its surface. When they follow the shape of the body, the mesh bends cleanly once it's rigged. In this long part you'll keep working on the mirrored base mesh: adding edge loops and moving vertices to round off the head and give it a nose, shape the neck, shoulders and chest, slim the ankles, rough in the feet, and shape the arms and wrists. At the end you'll apply the Mirror modifier. This isn't a character modeling exercise, so the aim is a reasonable shape, not a perfect one. Watch how the mesh is structured, and use anatomy references if you want to go further. The video uses Blender 2.90.

Add a nose
----------

1.  **Add a loop down the middle of the face.** <page-anchor target="#video-HtbduCFqVrs" value="260">4:20</page-anchor> A nose needs more edges. 2.  **Curve it outward.** <page-anchor target="#video-HtbduCFqVrs" value="289">4:49</page-anchor> The new loop is perfectly straight. Wrap-up
-------

The character now has edge loops where it needs to bend and edge flow that follows its shape, and it's one mesh, with the mirror applied. If it doesn't deform well once it's rigged, you can come back and change the geometry. Next you'll build the armature that will bend it.
