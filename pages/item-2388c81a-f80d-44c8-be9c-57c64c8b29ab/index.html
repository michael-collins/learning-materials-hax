When is a boolean operation a good choice, and what problems should you watch for?

Booleans are quick for cutting holes and combining shapes, especially while blocking out. Watch for messy n-gons, shading artifacts and non-manifold geometry, and clean up the topology or keep the boolean as a non-destructive modifier.
