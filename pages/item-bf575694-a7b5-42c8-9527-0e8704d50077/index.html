Combining processes: molds and casting from printed or cut masters; vacuum forming; vinyl cutting for graphics; finishing (sanding, priming, paint, sealing); embedding hardware and light (optional).

Ideas

Formative processes; the master and the copy; finishing as the difference between prototype and object; material sustainability (PLA, acrylic, MDF waste).
