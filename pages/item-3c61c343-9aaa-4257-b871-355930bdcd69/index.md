Getting 3D from 2D sheets: stacked sections, waffle/egg-crate, slotted assemblies, press-fit, living hinges, scoring and folding.

Ideas
-----

Sectioning, contouring, folding, tessellating (Iwamoto); tolerance and fit; friction fit vs. adhesives; material thickness as a design parameter; the parametric slot.

In class
--------

*   Lecture (30 min)
*   tolerance test kit demo (20 min)
*   press-fit exercise work session (90 min)
