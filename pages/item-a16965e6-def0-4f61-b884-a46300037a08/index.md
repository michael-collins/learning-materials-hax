Design a kit of no more than three part types that press-fit into at least two different structures. Slots must be parameterized to your measured kerf. No glue.

What to hand in
---------------

*   The kit (≥ 12 parts) and two assemblies, photographed
*   Source file with the slot parameter exposed

What you'll practise
--------------------

*   Design tolerance for a friction fit
*   Parameterize a slot

Time: 1 week
