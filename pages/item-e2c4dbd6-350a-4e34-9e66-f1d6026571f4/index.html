A brick texture looks stretched on one side of your model. What would you check, and how might you fix it?

Check the UV layout with a checker texture for stretched or distorted islands and uneven texel density. Fix it by adding or moving seams, re-unwrapping or relaxing those islands, and scaling them to a consistent density.
