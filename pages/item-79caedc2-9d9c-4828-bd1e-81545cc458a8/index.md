Course overview; shop tour and safety culture; what digital fabrication means for artists and designers; the file → machine → object loop.

Ideas
-----

Additive, subtractive, and formative process families; CAD → CAM → machine; the digital material (files as things); precision and repeatability; the workmanship of certainty vs. risk (Pye).

In class
--------

*   Intro lecture (40 min)
*   shop tour + safety orientation with the lab tech (60 min)
*   object autopsy kickoff (30 min)
