CGI Foundations is where every DART pathway starts. You will learn how 3D images are made, from polygons to pixels, and practice each stage of the pipeline twice: once on an airline chair and once on a room. The pathway ends with a project that brings modeling, texturing, lighting and rendering together in work of your own.

Check your readiness

Can you describe the main stages of a CGI project, from concept to final image?

Concept and reference, modeling, UV mapping and texturing (shading), lighting, camera and layout, rendering, and compositing or output. Animation joins the pipeline when things move.

What is the difference between modeling, texturing and lighting, and how does each affect the final render?

Modeling defines the shape, texturing and shading define what surfaces are made of and how they react to light, and lighting reveals the form, sets the mood and directs the viewer's eye.
