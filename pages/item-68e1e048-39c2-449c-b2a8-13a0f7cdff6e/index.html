Welcome to Getting Started with 3D Animation, a curated open educational textbook that guides you through the fundamentals and intermediate concepts of 3D animation and design.

This book pulls together lessons, articles, and tutorials from the OER Platform into a structured learning path. Each chapter references existing content that can also be explored independently.

How to Use This Book

Navigate through chapters using the sidebar or the prev/next buttons at the bottom of each page. The outline follows a progressive learning path, but you can jump to any chapter that interests you.

Prerequisites

No prior 3D experience is required. Basic computer skills and familiarity with creative software concepts will be helpful.
