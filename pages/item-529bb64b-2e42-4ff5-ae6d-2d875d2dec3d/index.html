In this exercise, you will apply Image textures to a Chess board and pieces

Learning Objectives

  1. Practice rendering images for submissions

Instructions

For this assignment, you will submit 3 total images.

Develop and refine a single chess set based on observation over time, peer and instructor feedback.

Submit three renders of this individual set to both Canvas and the corresponding discussion thread for review. 
Make sure that your assignment is titled in the following format: Last name, First name, the word "Lesson" followed by the Lesson number, and the word "Assignment" followed by the Assignment number.

Example: Brill Ian Lesson x Assignment x
