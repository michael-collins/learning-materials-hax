This pathway is designed for aspiring animation and VFX artists who want to create high-quality animated short films with professional-grade visual effects. Students will progress from foundational CGI skills through advanced animation and simulation techniques, culminating in the ability to produce complete animated narratives with integrated VFX elements.

Who This Is For

This pathway is ideal for students aiming to work in:

  • Animation studios
  • VFX houses
  • Film production companies
  • Independent animation projects
  • Post-production facilities

Career Outcomes

Graduates will be prepared for roles including:

  • Character Animator
  • VFX Artist
  • Compositor
  • Technical Director
  • Animation Generalist

Check your readiness

Outline the main stages of a short-film pipeline, from pre-production to post-production.

Story and script, storyboards and animatic, design, modeling and rigging, layout, animation, effects and simulation, lighting, rendering, compositing, then edit, sound and grade.

How would you make a simulated effect, like smoke or debris, feel part of an animated shot?

Match its lighting, scale, timing and motion to the shot, make it interact with the characters and set, and render it in passes so it can be balanced in compositing.
