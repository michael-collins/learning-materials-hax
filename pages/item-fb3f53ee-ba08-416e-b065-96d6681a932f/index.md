CGI Foundations is where every DART pathway starts. You will learn how 3D images are made, from polygons to pixels, and practice each stage of the pipeline twice: once on an airline chair and once on a room. The pathway ends with a project that brings modeling, texturing, lighting and rendering together in work of your own.

After Foundations
-----------------

Foundations is the prerequisite for every other pathway. On your next run of DART 303, choose a pathway that fits your interests, or explore them now to plan ahead.
