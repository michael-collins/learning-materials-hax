Five minutes, three slides, sources on the last slide. Sign up for a week that matches the process being introduced.

What to hand in

*   One paragraph added to the shared precedent list

What you'll practise
--------------------

*   Build the class's shared precedent bank

Time: 5 min, rotating weeks 3–13
