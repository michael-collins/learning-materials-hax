Each student presents one artist, designer, or studio and one specific work made with digital fabrication: how it was made (process, machine, material), why that process, and what it argues. Five minutes, three slides, sources on the last slide. Sign up for a week that matches the process being introduced.

What to hand in

  • 3-slide presentation with sources
  • One paragraph added to the shared precedent list

What you'll practise

  • Read a fabricated work technically
  • Build the class's shared precedent bank

Time: 5 min, rotating weeks 3–13
